We are an early-stage startup exploring ways to make food delivery more reliable, thoughtful, and accessible.

OUR PURPOSE

Make the delivery journey better.

Taste News is a technology-driven food delivery startup being developed in Davangere, Karnataka. We are exploring how thoughtful product innovation and digital technology can help improve the food delivery experience.

One of our key concepts is a Smart Thermal Delivery Bag intended to help manage food temperature during transit. The concept is under development, and its design, safety, and performance need to be validated through prototyping and testing.

Our long-term ambition is to begin locally and expand thoughtfully as the platform, product, and operating model mature.
